As part of Rice University's George R. Brown School of Engineering and Computing, we offer graduate and undergraduate degrees in computer science. Our graduate degrees are offered both in-person and online. In fact, our online master of science is ranked #3 by the US News & World Report in 2026.

We're one of the world's top CS departments, and that shows in what we do. Our faculty are thought leaders pushing cutting-edge research through partnerships with the Texas Medical Center, NASA and the local energy industry. They're also award-winning teachers who genuinely care about their students.

Whether you're a PhD student, a tenure-track faculty prospect, an undergrad or pursuing a Master's degree, we want you to know what makes Rice different. Our department is diverse and welcoming (34% of our undergrads are women), and we're based in Houston, consistently ranked as one of America's most diverse major cities. The campus feels like a park, especially in winter when you can actually be outside.

If you're considering joining us, come visit.

We think you'll see why Rice is home.
